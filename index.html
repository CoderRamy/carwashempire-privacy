Car Wash Empire - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Car Wash Empire (the "Game"), published by Crazy Beans ("we", "us"), handles information. The short version: we do not ask for your name, email or any account, and your game progress stays on your device. The only third-party service in the Game is Google AdMob, which shows optional video ads.

1. Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress (money, unlocked items, upgrade levels, play time and similar game state) locally on your device. This data is not sent to us. It is deleted when you clear the Game's data or uninstall the Game.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect, receive or store any personal information ourselves. The Game has no login, no accounts, no chat and no in-app purchases.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game can show optional rewarded video ads: an ad plays only when you tap a reward button such as "x2 earnings", "Free helper", "Repair now" or "Free soap". These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location;
  • information about your device, its operating system and the Game;
  • how you interact with ads (for example, whether an ad was viewed or tapped) and diagnostic data about ad performance.

Google processes this information under its own policies. You can learn more at Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Your choices and consent

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and wherever the law requires it, the Game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the Game's Settings screen (Ad privacy choices).
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (for example Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).
  • You never have to watch an ad to play the Game.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. Apart from the processing by Google AdMob described above, the Game does not share information with third parties.

6. Security

Data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. Game progress stays on your device.

7. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed. Continued use of the Game after a change means you accept the updated policy.
